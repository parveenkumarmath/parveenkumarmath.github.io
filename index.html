01 / Introduction

About me

I am a PhD Research Scholar in the Department of Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Technology Ropar, working under the supervision of Dr. Manmohan Vashisth.

My research focuses on inverse problems for partial differential equations. I am particularly interested in recovering unknown coefficients from boundary measurements and in establishing uniqueness and stability results for inverse coefficient identification problems.

The equations and systems I study include Schrödinger, heat, wave and related nonlinear PDEs.

02 / Research

Research interests

Some themes that guide my current research.

01

Inverse problems

Uniqueness, reconstruction and stability in the identification of unknown coefficients from boundary or partial boundary measurements.

Coefficient identification
02

Partial differential equations

Inverse problems for elliptic, parabolic and hyperbolic equations, including heat, Schrödinger and wave-type systems.

PDE analysis
03

Integral geometry

Ray transforms and related integral-geometric tools used to establish uniqueness and stability in inverse problems.

Geometric methods
04

Carleman estimates

Quantitative estimates and weighted inequalities for the analysis of PDEs and inverse coefficient problems.

Analytical techniques
05

Geometrical optics solutions

Construction and use of complex geometrical optics and geometric optics solutions in inverse boundary value problems.

Special solutions
06

Nonlinear inverse problems

Coefficient recovery in nonlinear PDEs using linearization ideas, higher-order interactions and boundary measurements.

Nonlinear analysis
